About This Property

Cozy Arts Dhangethi

A colourful, art-inspired guest house on Dhangethi in South Ari Atoll, celebrating local Maldivian craft and lacquer-work heritage — with whale sharks, manta rays and sandbanks just a short boat ride away.

Getting to Cozy Arts

Dhangethi is reached by speedboat from Maamigili domestic airport. Share your flight details with us in advance and we'll arrange your speedboat transfer and jetty pickup.
